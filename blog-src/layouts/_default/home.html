About Me

Hello! I'm Anirban Paul

I'm a Technical Consultant specializing in Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations (F&O). With hands-on experience in X++, C#, .NET, Azure, and SQL, I am experienced in designing and building enterprise-grade solutions for global clients.
